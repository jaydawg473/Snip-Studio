CHANGES
The normal editor window grows to the measured width needed by the capture
toolbar and horizontal annotation toolbar. Measurement uses the current scale
and control labels. The width is bounded by the current monitor's working area.
If the full toolbar cannot fit, one horizontal scrollbar keeps all controls
reachable in the same row with their complete widths. Left and right annotation
toolbars retain their vertical layout. Maximized/full-screen bounds are retained.
Changing tools and closing options do not automatically shrink an existing window.

The external-editor selector manages its own height and docks at the top of
a shrinkable settings row. Percentage-column preferred-size measurement had
left the closed picker much taller than its visible header. The prior signed
build reproduced 244 pixels of unused picker height at a normal font size;
the corrected picker has zero unused body height and its row returns to
45 pixels after closing. Expanded choices still push following settings down.
